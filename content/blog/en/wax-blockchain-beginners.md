## Gaming Needs a Different Blockchain

Ethereum and similar chains struggle with gaming due to high gas fees and slow transactions. For a bingo game where balls are drawn every few seconds, you need a blockchain designed for speed.

## Enter WAX

The Worldwide Asset eXchange (WAX) is a purpose-built blockchain for gaming and digital assets:

- **Zero gas fees**: Players never pay transaction costs
- **Carbon-neutral**: WAX is the first carbon-neutral blockchain
- **1.5-second finality**: Transactions confirm in under 2 seconds
- **680,000+ daily active users**: One of the most active gaming chains

## WAX for Players

If you're new to WAX, getting started takes 2 minutes:
1. Install Anchor Wallet or use WAX Cloud Wallet
2. Buy WAX from an exchange and transfer to your wallet
3. Visit CryptoBingo and connect — no bridging, no wrapping

## The WAX Ecosystem

WAX hosts thousands of dApps, NFT marketplaces, and games. The WUF token (used in CryptoBingo) is a WAX standard token, meaning it trades on all major WAX DEXes instantly.
