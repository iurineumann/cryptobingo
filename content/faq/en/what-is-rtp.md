---
question: "What is RTP and how is it calculated?"
category: "economy"
order: 4
---
RTP (Return to Player) is the percentage of buy-ins returned as prizes. CryptoBingo targets 85% RTP, meaning for every 100 WUF tokens wagered, approximately 85 WUF are paid back to players. The remaining 15% covers GGR (Gross Gaming Revenue) for operations, development, and token buyback/burn.
